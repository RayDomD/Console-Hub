Question fan plate

Decided design, 2026-08-31. Layer-1 instrument plate for the question fan — the first half of the console fusion work. Recorded by ADR 0007 and 0012–0014; see the console hub plan. Cascadia Mono and Anybody fall back to system faces in this file only. Hardening study: choose a rehearsal, focus Ask, and press Enter. Stop and close cut pending work immediately; failures preserve valid answers and expose a bounded recovery action.

Amended 2026-08-31 after the S6 grilling session. Three companion studies carry states this rehearsal does not animate: the criteria gate (the run holds between the criteria turn and the workers) and slot count (ghost card adds, a card-header × removes, both only while idle, 2-5 shown by controls appearing and vanishing). Also settled here: criteria may be typed by the user, drafted by the criteria slot, or skipped; slot model and effort are edited on the card itself while idle and frozen once running; a finished card keeps its scrollback until the plate closes, and that scrollback is not written to the architect's document. Presets were dropped - the arrangement persists on its own.

What this fixes. Sidebar is docked left: fan controls, then slots, then the existing skills[] registry rendered unchanged — one registry, two presentations. Reading order down the main column is causal: ask → criteria → four answers → comparison. Enter fires; there is no arm ceremony and no countdown, and Stop is an ordinary control. Criteria are written by a dedicated criteria slot before any worker starts, tool-less - never by the architect, which would grade against a rubric it wrote itself. The ordering guarantee holds regardless of any gate; the run nonetheless holds after the criteria turn so four workers never run against a scheme nobody read (ADR 0015, drawn in the criteria gate study). Colour carries identity only — a 2px cap on the card, a 3px bar in the sidebar, keyed to vendor rather than slot so two Anthropic slots share a hue and quota pressure reads at a glance. Effort stays achromatic in the existing glyph run, because effort is ordinal and colour encodes ordinal badly. Status stays achromatic. Wires stay achromatic.

Motion thesis. One mechanical relay: Ask takes a 1px key strike, criteria cut in from the leading edge, all four answer cards fan from one centre at the same instant, each finished card seats once, and the architect closes the circuit after all four are in. Nothing loops. Vendor hue never animates because it identifies ownership, not status. Buttons travel 1px under pointer pressure; Stop has no eased tail. Reduced motion removes travel but keeps state and causal delay.

Hardening thesis. A terminal slot fault cannot hold the merge open. Two or more valid answers may produce a visibly partial advisory comparison; fewer than two cannot compare. Retry runs only failed work, then replaces the partial comparison. Stop, close, and shutdown kill every pending process tree and retain completed output. A restored stale run is evidence, never a live process: it opens interrupted, ignores late events from the old run id, and can only restart as a new run.
