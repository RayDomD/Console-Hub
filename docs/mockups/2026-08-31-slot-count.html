What this changes in the decided design. grid-template-columns:repeat(4,1fr) becomes display:flex; flex-wrap:wrap; justify-content:center with cards fixed at 248px. Card header gains a × while idle, absent at two workers. A dashed + add slot follows the last card, absent at five. A new card starts on its vendor's default model and effort from the executors block — what resolveModelEffort already does for a slot that names nothing.

One cost of centring. Adding or removing a card re-centres the whole row, so every other card shifts sideways under the cursor. Left-alignment moves only the cards after the one you touched. It is a click or two, while idle, and never during a run — but it is the thing you would notice if you added three slots in a row.
