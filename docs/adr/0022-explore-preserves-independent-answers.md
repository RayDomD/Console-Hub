# Explore preserves independent answers

Explore sends the exact same Ask to every slot in a 2–5-slot Stack, in parallel, with read-only access to the selected Workspace. Slots cannot see one another's responses, the Orchestrator has no special turn, and Cockpit produces no synthesis. Every response remains separate and is shown with its model, duration, output tokens, and throughput; Direct is the Recipe for asking only one slot.
