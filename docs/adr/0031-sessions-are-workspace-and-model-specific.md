# Sessions are Workspace- and model-specific

A harnessed Session is keyed by slot, vendor model, and Workspace. Returning to that combination during one Cockpit launch resumes it, allowing Direct follow-ups and sequential Coordinate tasks to retain context without leaking one project's conversation into another or replaying history under a different model. Explore, Debate, and fresh Orchestrator turns still start clean where their Recipe contracts require independence.

Closing Cockpit discards agent-session memory, matching Fusion Harness. Branches, worktrees, results, and run evidence remain durable because the checkout is authority and memory is only an accelerator. Reset may clear one selected slot's Sessions or the entire Stack's Sessions. Consoles are separate interactive processes and are never resumable Sessions.
