# Debate closes with positions

Debate runs every Stack slot read-only against the same ask. Round one is independent; in each later round every surviving slot receives every other slot's complete, clearly attributed prior-round response and may defend, change position, combine compatible positions, or remain a minority while naming the evidence that moved it. The Orchestrator has no special authority, and Cockpit produces no judge, winner, or hidden synthesis.

Debate defaults to three rounds and permits two through five. A failed slot leaves later rounds; the run continues while at least two slots survive and otherwise stops with the reason visible.

**Status:** accepted
